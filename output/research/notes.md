## Historical building and present headquarters

KEK Building.jpg (Arianit, 13 September 2020, CC BY-SA 4.0) is retained in the restructuring section. The heritage inventory identifies the historical administrative tower as Elektrokosova (design dated 1977); KEDS's official 2022 access code gives its address as the Elektrokosova building, Bill Clinton Boulevard 5. The photograph displays KEDS/KESCO signs. The caption describes the historical administrative building and documented KEDS use, rather than calling it KEK's current headquarters.

Neither the precise interval when KEK used it as headquarters nor a property deed/asset-transfer date was established. Consequently no ownership-transfer or present KESCO ownership claim is made. The corporate registry dated 8 June 2026 and audited company notes instead establish KEK's registered headquarters at Nënë Tereza 36, Prishtina.

## Institutional dates

The public-enterprise registry gives 22 December 2005 for the shareholder decision/transformation and also calls it registration. Audited 2006 and 2025 notes give business registration on 27 December 2005. Dates are attributed in prose; the infobox states only “2005 (shoqëri aksionare)”. Plant commissioning is not treated as company incorporation.

KOSTT's audited notes separate incorporation on 23 December 2005 from independent operation on 1 July 2006. Licensing dates are not inferred from either event. KEDS's tender, agreement and operational handover are dated separately. KESCO supply is distinguished from KEDS distribution from January 2015.

Audited 2025 notes document KEK's newer commercial and last-resort supply activities. Modern KEK therefore cannot be described absolutely as never supplying customers. Its principal mining/generation role and the other companies' responsibilities remain clear.

## Capacity definitions

Historical installed capacities and dated regulatory net capacities occupy separate columns. A1/A2 are described as not operating; permanent legal decommissioning is not presumed. Historical Kosova A 800 MW includes those units; operating A3–A5 installed capacity is 610 MW.

A4: older ZRRE table and KEK's budget documentation give 1971 (KEK specifies 15 May 1971); recent ZRRE tables give 1974. The corroborated 1971 date is retained and discrepancy explicitly disclosed. A human editor may choose to shorten the explanatory note but should preserve the distinction.

The October 2025 government announcement gives B2 output of 318 MW without explicitly defining gross/net. It cannot silently replace the 264 MW regulatory net entry. Mechanical modernization is distinguished from delayed environmental filter/DeNOx retrofits.

A3 announcement metadata and strategy progress report establish January 2025, although the government page's body contains a 2024 date typo. Project value/target are announcements at prequalification, not completed operational capacity.

## Operational series

All generation table rows use ZRRE's plant-threshold series, after auxiliary consumption. No KEK gross figures are mixed into it. Older MWh are divided by 1,000; lignite thousand tonnes by 1,000 for million tonnes. Totals are calculated before rounding, so displayed components can differ by 0.1 GWh from the displayed total.

The original 5,284 GWh figure was a distribution-consumption label in the 2024 flow diagram, not KEK generation. Correct ZRRE 2024 plant-threshold total is 5,480.4 GWh. The report's gross narrative also contains an MWh/GWh typo; the unit table establishes approximately 6,178 GWh gross.

KEK and ZRRE totals are not always identical: 2024 KEK net 5,494.962 GWh versus ZRRE threshold 5,480.4; 2025 gross KEK 5,293.730 versus ZRRE 5,283.7 GWh, although 2025 net agrees within rounding (4,686.553 versus 4,686.5). No unsupported explanation of the metering/reporting difference is invented. The consistent regulator series is preferred.
