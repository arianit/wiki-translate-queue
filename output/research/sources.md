## Other sources inspected or rejected

- Original 2018 audit, [KEK audited financial statements 2018](https://me.rks-gov.net/njpmnp/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/06/KEK_Pasqyrat-Financiare-te-Audituara_2018-4.pdf), PDF 7 / printed 5. Earlier un-restated figures; later restated 2018 comparative preferred.
- KEK annual reports 2013 and 2024 inspected; original audited/regulatory figures preferred to unaudited annual summaries where available.
- The draft’s purported *Sustainable Energy Options for Kosovo* URL actually opens a large Inspection Panel report-and-annexes compilation, Report 69673-XK (June 2012). Its cover is not that title. Replaced with direct regulatory commissioning/capacity evidence and the contemporary World Bank 2003 appraisal.
- The draft’s World Bank “Environmental Assessment … Clean-up” URL is actually *Kosovo C: Strategic Environmental and Social Assessment*. Title corrected; clean-up results separately sourced to the dated 2015 snapshot.
- The draft’s IEG privatization-criticism page returned HTTP 403 through both direct and browser access, preventing independent passage verification. Unsupported summary removed; no claim of absence of criticism.
- Lajmi.net repetition of KEK’s 2024 performance release replaced with ZRRE annual data. Kosovo Projects solar summary replaced with original EIB financing/procurement records.
- Generic ministry media files titled Raporti_Pasqyrave_Financiare_2024 sometimes refer to Telekom/Posta, not KEK. These were rejected and never used for KEK figures.
